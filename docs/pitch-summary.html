Investor Q&A

Why is now the right time for an AI buying agent?

LLM capabilities have reached the inflection point where autonomous purchasing decisions are viable. Claude 3.5 offers the reasoning depth, low hallucination rates, and Portuguese fluency required. Combined with our existing marketplace infrastructure and supplier relationships, we can deploy agentic capabilities that weren't possible 18 months ago.

How do you prevent AI errors in purchasing?

We implement graduated autonomy with human-in-the-loop workflows. The agent starts with suggestions, graduates to auto-execute within approval limits, and always escalates edge cases. Every action is logged, reversible, and constrained by pharmacy-defined rules. We're building trust through transparency.

What's your moat against big tech or incumbents?

Three layers: (1) Deep supplier integrations that took years to build, (2) Proprietary transaction data that trains our models, (3) Vertical-specific knowledge (ANVISA regulations, pharma supply chain nuances). A generic AI can't replicate domain expertise + data + relationships.

How do you control LLM costs at scale?

Semantic caching for repeated queries, tiered models (Haiku for simple tasks, Sonnet for complex), query cost estimation, and monthly budget caps per pharmacy. Our architecture is LLM-vendor agnostic, so we can optimize costs as the market evolves.

What's the path to 40% agent-assisted orders?

Phase 1: Analytics dashboards (build trust). Phase 2: Buying suggestions (prove value). Phase 3: Auto-reorder for replenishment items (low risk). Phase 4: Full procurement automation for willing pharmacies. Each phase expands the trust boundary.

How do suppliers benefit from this?

Suppliers get: (1) Predictable demand signals for inventory planning, (2) Direct channel to pharmacies without sales rep overhead, (3) Data on what drives purchasing decisions. The agent optimizes for pharmacy value, but suppliers win through volume and efficiency.

The Narrative

The Problem We're Solving

Every week, pharmacy owners across Brazil spend 15+ hours on procurement. They check prices across a dozen suppliers, compare promotions, negotiate payment terms, and manually place orders. It's tedious, error-prone, and takes them away from what matters: serving their customers and growing their business.

The pharmaceutical supply chain in Brazil is fragmented. Manufacturers, distributors, and pharmacies operate in silos. There's no unified view of pricing, inventory, or demand. Every pharmacy reinvents the wheel, making the same comparisons their neighbors made yesterday.

What We've Built

Cienty started as a B2B marketplace connecting pharmacies with suppliers. But we realized the marketplace was just the foundation. The real value is in the infrastructure we've built underneath:

  • Deep supplier integrations — Direct connections to manufacturers and distributors, with real-time pricing and inventory data
  • Unified data layer — Order history, demand patterns, promotions, and payment terms all in one place
  • Agentic AI architecture — Purpose-built for autonomous decision-making, not a chatbot bolted onto a legacy system
"We're not building another marketplace. We're building the AI that runs pharmacy procurement."

The Buying Agent Vision

Imagine a pharmacy owner who says: "Cienty, restock my store."

The agent analyzes inventory levels and demand patterns. It scans all connected suppliers for the best prices. It considers promotions, payment terms, and delivery times. It optimizes the order across multiple suppliers to minimize cost and maximize availability. Then it executes — within the approval limits the owner has set.

The owner reviews a summary, approves with one tap, and gets back to running their business. What used to take hours now takes seconds.

Why We Win

Our moat is the combination of three things that are hard to replicate:

  • Data network effects — Every transaction makes the agent smarter for all pharmacies. Pricing intelligence, demand forecasting, supplier reliability scores — they all improve with scale.
  • Deep integrations — We've spent years building direct connections with suppliers. A new entrant can't replicate these relationships overnight.
  • Vertical expertise — We understand ANVISA regulations, LGPD compliance, Brazilian Portuguese nuances, and the quirks of pharmaceutical logistics. Generic AI solutions miss these details.
